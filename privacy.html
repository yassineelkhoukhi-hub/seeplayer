Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SEE PLAYER TV, provided by Mempas Deve (“we”, “our”, or “the developer”), handles information when you use the app.

1. What the app does

SEE PLAYER TV is a media player that supports user-provided compatible playlists, including M3U playlists. The app can use playlist addresses or files supplied by the user to display and play the content referenced by those playlists.

2. Playlist information and network connections

When you add or use a playlist, the app may access the playlist source and media locations specified by you. Those external servers may receive technical connection information, such as your device’s IP address, as part of normal internet communication. The privacy practices of those providers are governed by their own policies, not this one.

Only add playlists and media sources that you trust and are authorized to use. We do not provide playlist content through this website.

3. Personal information and analytics

The developer has not yet published a verified inventory of all data handled by the app and its bundled components. Before relying on this policy for a store submission, the developer must check the app’s source code, permissions, network requests, and third-party libraries and update this section to accurately disclose any data collected, stored, shared, or processed. Do not assume that no data is collected until that review is complete.

4. Children

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to the developer through the app, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

5. Security and retention

Playlist data may be stored or handled on your device depending on the app’s implementation. The developer should specify retention and deletion behavior after verifying the current app build. Use trusted playlist sources and protect access to your device.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The revised date will be shown at the top of this page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Mempas Deve at ferasseednane@gmail.com.
